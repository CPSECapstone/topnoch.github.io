We value your feedback! During the alpha phase, your reports help us improve NOCH faster.

## Reporting Channels

| Channel | Use For | Link |
|---------|---------|------|
| GitHub Issues | Bugs, defects, regressions | [Open an Issue](https://github.com/CPSECapstone/noch/issues) |

---

## How to Report a Bug

When filing a bug report, please include:

1. **App version and platform** (Android / iOS / Web)
2. **Device model and OS version** (e.g., Pixel 7, Android 14)
3. **Exact steps to reproduce** the issue
4. **Expected behavior** — what should have happened
5. **Actual behavior** — what actually happened
6. **Screenshots, recordings, or logs** (if available)

## Feature Requests

We welcome feature ideas! Send them via email with the subject line:

**`FEATURE: <short title>`**

Please include:
- Who would benefit (user persona)
- What problem it solves
- Your desired outcome
