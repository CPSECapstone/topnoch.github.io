# NOCH Privacy Policy (GDPR Compliant)

**Last updated:** April 15, 2026

The NOCH Team ("NOCH", "we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our application and website.

---

## What Data Do We Collect?

NOCH collects the following data:

- **Personal identification information:** Name, email address, phone number, username
- **Profile and social content:** Photos, posts, bio, media uploads
- **Usage and diagnostic data:** App interactions, feature usage patterns
- **Technical metadata:** IP address, device model, operating system, app version
- **Optional location data** (with your explicit permission) for nearby restaurant features
- **Reservation and booking data:** Restaurant selections, booking dates and times

## How Do We Collect Your Data?

You directly provide NOCH with most of the data we collect. We collect and process data when you:

- Register online or create an account on the NOCH platform
- Make a reservation or interact with restaurant listings
- Create posts, upload media, or engage with social features
- Voluntarily complete a survey or provide feedback via email or our issue tracker
- Use or view our application, which may collect technical metadata automatically

NOCH may also receive your data indirectly from:

- Third-party authentication providers (e.g., Google Sign-In, Apple ID) if you choose to sign in with them
- Analytics and crash-reporting services used to improve app stability

### Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

Where applicable, our legal bases for processing include:

- **Contract performance** — to provide you with the services you signed up for
- **Legitimate interests** — to maintain and improve the platform
- **Consent** — where you have opted into optional features (e.g., location, marketing)
- **Legal obligations** — where we are required by law to retain or disclose data

## How Will We Use Your Data?

NOCH collects your data so that we can:

- Process your reservations and manage your account
- Provide core social features (feed, messaging, profiles)
- Improve the reliability, safety, and quality of the platform
- Respond to support requests and operational incidents
- Send you important updates about the service

**We do not sell your personal data.**

If you agree, NOCH may send you information about new features or related services. You can opt out at any time.

## How Do We Store Your Data?

NOCH securely stores your data in managed cloud systems (Firebase / Google Cloud Platform) with industry-standard access controls and encryption.

We retain your data only as long as necessary for the purposes described above, or as required by law. When data is no longer needed, it will be deleted or anonymized.

## Marketing

NOCH may send you information about product updates, features, and services we think you would find useful.

If you have agreed to receive marketing communications, you may always opt out at a later date. You have the right at any time to stop NOCH from contacting you for marketing purposes.

To opt out, email us at [noch-support@cpsecapstone.org](mailto:noch-support@cpsecapstone.org) with the subject line "Unsubscribe."

## What Are Your Data Protection Rights?

NOCH would like to make sure you are fully aware of your data protection rights. Every user is entitled to the following:

- **The right to access** — You have the right to request copies of your personal data. We may charge a small fee for this service.
- **The right to rectification** — You have the right to request that NOCH correct any information you believe is inaccurate or complete information you believe is incomplete.
- **The right to erasure** — You have the right to request that NOCH erase your personal data, under certain conditions.
- **The right to restrict processing** — You have the right to request that NOCH restrict the processing of your personal data, under certain conditions.
- **The right to object to processing** — You have the right to object to NOCH's processing of your personal data, under certain conditions.
- **The right to data portability** — You have the right to request that NOCH transfer the data we have collected to another organization, or directly to you, under certain conditions.

If you make a request, we have one month to respond to you. If you would like to exercise any of these rights, please contact us:

- **Email:** [noch-support@cpsecapstone.org](mailto:noch-support@cpsecapstone.org)
- **GitHub Issues:** [https://github.com/CPSECapstone/noch/issues](https://github.com/CPSECapstone/noch/issues)

## What Are Cookies?

Cookies are text files placed on your computer to collect standard Internet log information and visitor behavior information. When you visit our website, we may collect information from you automatically through cookies or similar technology.

For further information, visit [allaboutcookies.org](https://allaboutcookies.org).

## How Do We Use Cookies?

NOCH uses cookies in a range of ways to improve your experience, including:

- Keeping you signed in
- Understanding how you use our website
- Remembering your preferences

## What Types of Cookies Do We Use?

Our website uses the following cookies:

- **Functionality** — We use these cookies to recognize you and remember your preferences (language, location, etc.). A mix of first-party and third-party cookies are used.
- **Analytics** — We use these cookies to understand how visitors interact with our website, helping us improve the experience.

## How to Manage Cookies

You can set your browser not to accept cookies. Visit [allaboutcookies.org](https://allaboutcookies.org) for instructions on how to remove cookies from your browser. However, some website features may not function as a result.

## Privacy Policies of Other Websites

The NOCH website may contain links to other websites. Our privacy policy applies only to our platform, so if you click on a link to another website, you should read their privacy policy.

## Changes to Our Privacy Policy

NOCH keeps its privacy policy under regular review and places any updates on this page. This privacy policy was last updated on **April 15, 2026**.

## How to Contact Us

If you have any questions about NOCH's privacy policy, the data we hold on you, or you would like to exercise one of your data protection rights, please do not hesitate to contact us:

- **GitHub Issues:** [https://github.com/CPSECapstone/noch/issues](https://github.com/CPSECapstone/noch/issues)

## How to Contact the Appropriate Authority

Should you wish to report a complaint or if you feel that NOCH has not addressed your concern in a satisfactory manner, you may contact your local data protection authority. In the EU, you can find your authority at [edpb.europa.eu](https://edpb.europa.eu/about-edpb/about-edpb/members_en).
